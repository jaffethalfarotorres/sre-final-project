Explain it to a student who is new to IT:
1. One everyday comparison.
2. Two sentences on what it is and why SREs care. If it is useful at work, add one line on where a junior SRE or DevOps engineer meets it on the job.
3. Where it lives in this project (file or dashboard) and one read-only command or link to see it.
4. One question to check they understood.
