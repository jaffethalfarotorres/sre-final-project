description: Open Mission Control, the visual map of your climb to On-Call Ready
---
Run `python3 scripts/lab.py play` (Windows: `python scripts\lab.py play`). It writes `.lab/mission-control.html` and opens it in the browser. Inside Bob the student can also open it with Command Palette > **Simple Browser: Show** and the file:/// link the command prints.

Walk the student through it in four short lines: where Bob stands on the mountain (current camp and level), what Nimbus Store can do now, the next mission card, and the achievements still to earn.
